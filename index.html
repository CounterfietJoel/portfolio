A Bit of Context

Why an engineering professor is designing your pitch decks.

Who I Am

I teach robotics, industrial automation, and AI to engineering students. Having a background in Mechatronics and research in Additive Manufacturing (3D printing) means I understand the exact physics, code, and CAD behind technical products.

I bring that technical depth into information design: helping deep-tech founders, research labs, and hardware startups transform dense engineering schematics and data into clear, persuasive, investor-ready pitch decks and visual narratives.
